## Try more real videos

One real phone video has worked so far, of a metal door knob. The knob came out recognisable, but the flat door was slightly bumpy and only about 190 points per frame were matched. Try a matte object, and use the results to retune the photo check limits. Frames hold less detail than photos, and phone stabilization, autofocus, rolling shutter and HDR can all reduce quality.
